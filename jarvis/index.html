Jarvis Read-Only Assistant

A personal assistant operated by Chad Reddy to help him review his own mail and calendar.

What it does

With the owner's permission, Jarvis reads Gmail messages and Google Calendar information to help review mail, understand upcoming events and prepare personal summaries.

This Google OAuth integration requests read-only access. It cannot send, change or delete emails, or create, change or delete calendar events through those permissions.

Private by purpose

This integration is for the owner's own accounts, not a public sign-up service or a client-data service. Mail and calendar information is not published, shared with other users, sold or used for advertising.

This information website has no sign-in, forms, analytics or access to mail, calendars or credentials.

Contact

Operator: Chad Reddy. For general questions, open a public support issue. Do not include emails, calendar details, personal information or credentials. The Google consent screen also provides the owner's selected support email for private enquiries.
